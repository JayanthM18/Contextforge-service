# Sizing

All measurements on the size chart are **body measurements, not garment
measurements**. Measure your chest at the fullest point, your waist at the
natural waist, and your inseam from crotch to floor without shoes.

## Between sizes

Size up for insulated layers you plan to wear over a fleece and for anything
described as *Athletic fit*. Size down for base layers, which are cut to be
worn next to skin. Our *Relaxed fit* trousers already include room for a base
layer.

## Footwear

Boot sizes run a half size small across the range. Measure in the afternoon,
when feet are at their largest, and allow a thumb's width of space in front of
the toe when standing. Wide fittings are available in the Cascade and Ridgeline
boots only.

## Kids

Children's sizes are by age band with a generous cut, expecting a season of
growth. Where a child sits between bands, choose the larger.

## If the size is wrong

Sizing is the most common reason for a return and an exchange is usually
faster. Use the advance exchange described in `exchanges.md` rather than
refunding and reordering, because it ships the replacement before we receive
the original.
