# Shipping and delivery

Orders placed before 14:00 local warehouse time on a business day are picked
the same day. Orders placed after that cut-off, at a weekend, or on a public
holiday are picked on the next business day.

## Delivery speeds and cost

| Service | Delivery estimate | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | Free over $75, otherwise $6.95 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Next Day | Next business day if ordered before 14:00 | $24.95 |

Delivery estimates count business days from dispatch, not from when the order
was placed. A Standard order placed on a Friday afternoon typically dispatches
on Monday and arrives the following Thursday or Friday.

## Where we ship

We ship to street addresses and carrier pickup points in the United States,
Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. We do not deliver to PO
boxes, and we cannot ship hazardous goods such as camping gas canisters or
bear spray by air, so those items are restricted to Standard ground delivery
within the contiguous United States.

## Split shipments

When one part of an order is on backorder we ship the available items
immediately at no extra charge and send the remainder when stock arrives. You
are charged shipping once.
