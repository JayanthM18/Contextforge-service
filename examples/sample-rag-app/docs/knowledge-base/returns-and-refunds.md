# Returns and refunds

Northwind Supply Co. accepts returns on most items within **30 days of
delivery**. The 30-day window starts on the date the carrier marks the parcel
as delivered, not the date the order was placed.

To qualify for a refund an item must be unused, in its original packaging, and
accompanied by the order number. Items that show signs of wear, are missing
tags, or have been washed are not eligible.

## How to start a return

Open the Returns Centre in your account, select the order, and choose the items
you are sending back. You will receive a prepaid return label by email within
one business hour. Attach the label to the outside of the parcel and drop it at
any carrier access point.

## When the money arrives

Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the warehouse has
inspected the returned item. Inspection takes up to 3 business days from the
moment the parcel is scanned in. After inspection the refund is released
immediately, but the card issuer may take a further 5 to 10 business days to
post it to your statement.

## Non-returnable items

Perishable food, climbing rope cut to a custom length, personalised engraving,
and any item marked *Final Sale* on the product page cannot be returned.
Gift cards are also non-returnable. See `gift-cards.md` for the separate rules
that cover unused gift card balances.
