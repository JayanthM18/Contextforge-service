# International orders

We operate storefronts for the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and
the European Union. Each storefront prices in its local currency, holds its own
stock and ships domestically within its region.

## Choosing a storefront

The storefront is chosen at checkout by the delivery address, not by your
location. A visitor in Germany sending a gift to a Canadian address checks out
on the Canadian storefront and is billed in Canadian dollars.

## What differs by region

Assortment differs: some brands are not licensed for sale in every region.
Prices differ because they include the local tax treatment. The return window
is 30 days everywhere, but EU customers also have the statutory 14-day right of
withdrawal, which runs in parallel and is not reduced by our policy.

## Delivery times

International delivery estimates assume clearance is uneventful. Customs delays
are outside carrier control and do not count toward the 5-day "tracking has not
moved" rule in `order-tracking.md`.

## What we cannot do

We cannot ship an order across storefronts, split one order across two
countries, or exchange an item bought in one region for delivery in another.
Duties and taxes are covered in `customs-and-duties.md`.
