# Tracking an order

Every dispatched parcel gets a tracking number. You will find it in the
dispatch confirmation email and on the order page in your account.

## Status meanings

- **Processing** — the order is accepted but has not left the warehouse. You
  can still cancel it at this stage; see `cancellations.md`.
- **Dispatched** — the carrier has collected the parcel and the tracking number
  is live. Tracking can take up to 6 hours to show its first scan.
- **In transit** — the parcel is moving through the carrier network.
- **Out for delivery** — the parcel is on a vehicle and will be attempted today.
- **Delivered** — the carrier has recorded a successful delivery.

## Tracking has not updated

Carriers sometimes go quiet for 24 to 48 hours in the middle of a journey,
particularly on international routes where the parcel is between networks. This
is normal. If tracking has not moved for **5 consecutive business days**, treat
the parcel as lost and contact support; see `damaged-or-missing-items.md`.

## Marked delivered but not received

Check with neighbours and around the property first, as carriers often leave
parcels in a safe place and photograph them. If it has not appeared within 24
hours of the delivery scan, report it and we will open a carrier investigation.
