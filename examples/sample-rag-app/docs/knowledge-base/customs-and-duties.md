# Customs, duties and taxes

Prices on each regional storefront include that region's consumption tax. US
prices exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout from the delivery
address.

## Delivered duty paid

Orders shipped within the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or the
European Union from their own regional warehouse are **delivered duty paid**.
You pay nothing further on arrival and the carrier will not ask you for money.

If a carrier demands a payment on a domestic order, do not pay it — it is
either a misclassification or a scam. Photograph the notice and send it to
support.

## Orders that cross a border

A small number of specialist items ship direct from a supplier outside your
region. These are flagged *Ships internationally* on the product page and are
**delivered duty unpaid**: the carrier collects import duty and handling before
release. The amount is set by your customs authority, not by us, and we cannot
estimate it.

## Duties on a return

Import duty paid on a cross-border order is refundable by your customs
authority, not by us. We refund the item price and, where the return is due to
a defect, the original shipping. Reclaiming duty is a form you file yourself;
keep the carrier's paperwork.
