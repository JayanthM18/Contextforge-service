# Bulk and business orders

Orders of **12 or more units** of a single item, or any order above $2,500,
qualify for business pricing. Submit the request through the Business Orders
form rather than checking out normally; a quote follows within 2 business days.

## What business pricing includes

Tiered discounts starting at 10% for 12 units and reaching 25% at 100 units,
free Standard delivery at any value, consolidated invoicing, and payment terms
of net 30 for approved accounts. Custom embroidery and logo application are
available with a 15-unit minimum.

## Stock and lead time

Business orders draw on reserved stock rather than the consumer pool, so
availability shown on the website is not a reliable guide. Typical lead time is
5 business days for stocked items and 4 to 6 weeks where a production run is
needed. Custom decoration adds 10 business days.

## Returns on business orders

Business orders are **not covered by the 30-day consumer return policy** in
`returns-and-refunds.md`. Returns are by prior agreement only, within 14 days
of delivery, and a 15% restocking fee applies to unopened cases. Decorated and
custom items cannot be returned at all.

## Tax exemption

Registered tax-exempt organisations submit their certificate once; it is
applied to all future orders on the account.
