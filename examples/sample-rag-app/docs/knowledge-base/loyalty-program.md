# Trailhead loyalty points

Every account earns **1 point per dollar** spent, excluding shipping, tax and
gift card purchases. Northwind Plus members earn 2 points per dollar; see
`subscription-plans.md`.

## Redeeming

100 points is worth $5 off an order. Points are redeemed in blocks of 100 at
checkout and can be combined with a gift card but not with a percentage-off
promotion code.

## When points arrive

Points post **14 days after delivery**, not at purchase. The delay covers the
return window, so points are not earned on items that come straight back. A
refund removes the points that item earned; if they have already been spent,
the balance goes negative and future earnings pay it down.

## Expiry

Points expire after **24 months of account inactivity**. Any order, return or
sign-in resets the clock. You are emailed 30 days before expiry.

## Tiers

Spending $500 in a calendar year reaches Summit tier, which adds free Express
delivery and a dedicated support queue. Tier is assessed on 1 January and held
for the whole of the following year, so a strong year protects you through a
quiet one.
