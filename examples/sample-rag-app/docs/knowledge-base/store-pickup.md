# Store and locker pickup

Pickup is available at the 14 Northwind stores and at roughly 3,000 carrier
locker points across our regions. Pickup is free regardless of order value.

## How it works

Choose *Collect* at checkout and pick a location. You are emailed a collection
code when the order is ready — usually the next business day for store stock
and 2 to 3 business days for warehouse stock. Bring the code and photo
identification.

## Collection window

Orders are held for **10 calendar days** for store pickup and **3 calendar
days** at a locker, which is a carrier limit we cannot extend. Uncollected
orders are returned to the warehouse and refunded in full automatically,
including any shipping paid.

## Collection by someone else

Name a nominated collector at checkout. They need the code and their own photo
identification. We cannot release an order to someone who is not named, even
with the code.

## Returns at a store

Any item can be returned at a Northwind store within the normal 30-day window,
including items originally delivered to an address. Bring the order number.
Store returns are inspected on the spot, so the refund is released the same day
rather than after warehouse inspection (`returns-and-refunds.md`).
