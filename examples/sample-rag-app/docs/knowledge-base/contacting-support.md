# Contacting support

## Channels and hours

Live chat runs 07:00 to 22:00 local time, seven days a week, and is the fastest
channel. Email is answered within **one business day**; complex cases involving
a carrier investigation or a warranty decision take longer. Phone support runs
09:00 to 17:00 on business days. There is no support on 25 December or
1 January.

## What to have ready

The order number is the single most useful thing. With it, most questions are
answered in one message. Without it, we need the email address the order was
placed with plus the delivery postcode, and identity checks add time.

## Escalation

If a case has been open for more than 5 business days without a resolution, ask
for it to be escalated. Escalated cases go to a senior agent with authority to
issue a goodwill refund or replacement without a carrier decision.

## What support cannot do

Support cannot change the delivery address after dispatch, cannot tell you why
a bank declined a card (`payment-methods.md`), cannot reverse a completed
carrier delivery, and cannot extend the 30-day return window except where
Northwind Plus applies (`subscription-plans.md`).
