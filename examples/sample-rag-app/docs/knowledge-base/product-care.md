# Caring for your gear

## Waterproof shells

Wash a shell when water stops beading on the face fabric. Use a technical wash,
never household detergent, and never fabric softener — both leave residues that
attract water. Machine wash warm at 30°C on a gentle cycle, rinse twice, then
tumble dry on low for 20 minutes to reactivate the durable water repellent
finish. Reproofing is needed roughly every 10 washes.

## Down insulation

Wash down with a down-specific soap on a gentle cycle and dry with three clean
tennis balls on low heat. Expect two to three hours of drying. Down that is
stored compressed loses loft permanently, so store bags and jackets loose in a
cotton sack.

## Boots

Brush off mud before it dries, dry away from direct heat, and never on a
radiator — heat cracks leather and delaminates soles. Condition full-grain
leather every 20 days of use.

## Climbing rope

Retire any rope that has held a factor-2 fall, shows core exposure, or is
10 years old from manufacture regardless of use. Wash in cool water with no
detergent and dry flat out of sunlight.

Care failures are not warranty defects; see `warranty.md`.
