# Damaged, wrong or missing items

Report any of these within **7 days of delivery** so we can hold the carrier
accountable. Reports after 7 days are still investigated but the carrier will
usually refuse the claim, which limits what we can recover.

## Damaged in transit

Photograph the outer packaging before you unpack anything else, then the item.
The packaging photograph is what carriers accept as evidence. We ship a
replacement immediately and send a prepaid label for the damaged item; you do
not wait for the return to arrive.

## Wrong item received

Keep the item and the packing slip and report it. We ship the correct item and
collect the wrong one. You are never asked to pay the difference if the item we
sent by mistake is more expensive.

## Missing from a multi-item parcel

Check the packing slip first: a split shipment (`shipping-and-delivery.md`)
means the remainder is still in transit and the slip will say so. If the slip
lists an item that is not in the box, report it as missing.

## Parcel never arrived

Follow the lost-parcel rule in `order-tracking.md`. Once a carrier
investigation is opened, a decision takes up to 8 business days, after which we
refund or replace at your choice.
