# Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and
Northwind gift cards. We do not accept cheques, bank transfer, cryptocurrency
or cash on delivery.

## When you are charged

Placing an order creates an **authorisation**, which reserves the funds but
does not take them. The charge is **captured** when the parcel is dispatched.
For a split shipment each parcel is captured separately, so your statement may
show two smaller charges rather than one.

An authorisation that is not captured within 7 days expires and the funds are
released by your bank automatically.

## Paying with more than one method

A gift card can be combined with one card or wallet. The gift card balance is
consumed first and the remainder goes to the second method. Two credit cards
cannot be combined on a single order.

## Declined payments

The most common causes are an address mismatch with the card issuer, an expired
card, and a daily spending limit. We are not told the specific reason a bank
declines a transaction, so if a card is repeatedly refused the issuer is the
only source of the answer. The order stays in Processing for 48 hours to give
you time to change the payment method before it is cancelled.
