# Northwind Plus subscription

Northwind Plus costs **$49 per year** or $5.50 per month. It includes free
Express delivery with no minimum order value, double loyalty points, a 60-day
return window instead of 30, and early access to sale events 24 hours before
they open publicly.

## Free trial

New members get a 30-day free trial, once per household. The trial converts to
a paid plan automatically unless cancelled, and you are told by email 3 days
before it converts.

## Billing

Annual plans are billed on the anniversary of sign-up. Monthly plans are billed
on the same day each month; where that day does not exist in a shorter month,
billing moves to the last day of the month.

## Cancelling

Cancel at any time in Account Settings. Benefits continue until the end of the
period you have already paid for and the plan does not renew. We do not
pro-rate a partial year: cancelling an annual plan in month two still gives you
the remaining ten months of benefits, with no refund.

## Interaction with the return window

The 60-day Plus return window applies to orders placed while the membership was
active. Letting a membership lapse does not shorten the window on orders that
were already placed.
