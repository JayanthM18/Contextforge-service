# Price matching and price drops

## Matching a competitor

We match the advertised price of an identical item — same brand, model, colour
and size — sold new and in stock by an authorised retailer that ships to your
region. Send a link before you order and we apply the difference as a discount.

We do not match marketplace sellers, auction listings, membership-only pricing,
clearance of discontinued stock, bundle pricing, or prices that include a
competitor's own loyalty credit.

## Our own price drops

If an item you bought drops in price within **14 days of your order date**, we
refund the difference to the original payment method. Request it through
support with the order number. This applies once per item and is not available
on items bought during a sitewide sale event.

## Sale events

Prices during Black Friday, end-of-season clearance and Plus early access are
final for the duration of the event. Items bought at full price in the 14 days
before an event **are** eligible for the price drop refund, which is the main
reason to buy early rather than wait.

## Interaction with returns

A price adjustment does not restart the 30-day return window in
`returns-and-refunds.md`. The window still runs from the original delivery date.
