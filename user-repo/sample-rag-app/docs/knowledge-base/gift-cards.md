# Gift cards

Digital gift cards are delivered by email within 15 minutes of purchase and are
available from $25 to $500. Physical gift cards ship as a normal order and
follow the delivery times in `shipping-and-delivery.md`.

## Using a card

Enter the 16-digit code at checkout. The balance is applied before any card or
wallet payment. A card can be split across as many orders as you like until the
balance reaches zero.

## Expiry

Gift card balances **do not expire**. Promotional credit issued as an apology or
as part of a campaign is different: it expires 12 months after issue and the
expiry date is shown in your account.

## Refunds involving gift cards

When an order paid partly by gift card is refunded, the gift card portion goes
back to the gift card and the remainder to the original payment method. A gift
card balance is never refunded as cash.

## Lost cards and fraud

A digital card can be reissued to the original purchaser if it has not been
redeemed. We cannot reissue a redeemed card, and we cannot tell you who
redeemed it. Gift cards themselves cannot be returned or refunded, which is why
they appear in the non-returnable list in `returns-and-refunds.md`.
