# Cancelling an order

An order can be cancelled by you, without contacting anyone, for as long as it
is in **Processing** status. Open the order in your account and choose *Cancel
order*. The authorisation on your card is released immediately, though your
bank may take a few days to remove the pending charge.

## After dispatch

Once an order reaches **Dispatched** it can no longer be cancelled, because the
parcel has physically left the warehouse. Refuse the delivery at the door or
return the item under the normal 30-day policy in `returns-and-refunds.md`.

## Partial cancellation

You can cancel individual lines of a multi-item order while it is in
Processing. Cancelling enough items to drop the order below the $75 free
shipping threshold adds the $6.95 Standard shipping fee to the remaining
items.

## Cancellations we make

We cancel an order ourselves when stock cannot be fulfilled, when an address
fails carrier validation, or when a payment authorisation is declined on
capture. You are told by email within one business day and never charged. A
price error on the product page is also grounds for cancellation; we honour the
listed price only where the error was not obvious.
