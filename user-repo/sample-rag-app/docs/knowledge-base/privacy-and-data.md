# Privacy and your data

We collect the data needed to take an order and run an account: name, delivery
and billing address, email, phone number, order history and support
correspondence. Payment card numbers are handled by our payment processor and
never reach Northwind systems.

## Marketing

Marketing email requires explicit opt-in and every message carries an
unsubscribe link that takes effect within 48 hours. Transactional messages —
order confirmation, dispatch, delivery, returns — are not marketing and cannot
be switched off while an order is open.

## Retention

Order records are kept for **7 years** to meet tax and accounting obligations.
Support conversations are kept for 24 months. Marketing preferences are kept
until you change them.

## Access and deletion

You can request a copy of your data or its deletion from Account Settings. A
copy arrives within 30 days. Deletion removes the account, addresses, marketing
preferences and support history, but **not** order records inside the 7-year
retention period, which are retained in a restricted form that is not linked to
a live account.

## Sharing

We share addresses with carriers to deliver, and transaction data with the
payment processor and with tax authorities where required. We do not sell
personal data.
