# Warranty

Northwind-branded gear carries a **2-year limited warranty** against defects in
materials and workmanship, starting on the delivery date. Third-party brands
sold by Northwind carry whatever warranty their manufacturer offers, which is
stated on the product page.

## What is covered

Seam failure, delamination, zip and buckle failure, broken frames, and coatings
that fail under normal use are covered. We repair where a repair restores the
item, and replace where it does not. If the exact product is discontinued we
substitute the closest current equivalent.

## What is not covered

Normal wear, UV fading, abrasion from rock or bark, damage from improper
storage, accidental damage, and any modification to the product. Items bought
second-hand or from an unauthorised reseller are outside the warranty
altogether.

## Making a claim

Submit photographs of the defect and the original order number through the
Warranty Claim form. A decision takes up to 10 business days. Approved claims
ship the repair or replacement free; you pay nothing for return postage.

## Warranty is not a return

A warranty claim is about a defect and has no time limit beyond the 2 years. A
return is about changing your mind and closes after 30 days
(`returns-and-refunds.md`). Do not use a return to report a defect that appears
in month four — you will be refused the return and should claim instead.
